The Question That Works Every Time

Life Skills · Online · Page 3 of 6

DIRECTIONS: Ask this of all seven. Fill in the last column.
An advert is not a lie. The scooter might really be the best one.
But somebody paid to be enthusiastic would have been enthusiastic anyway, so their opinion weighs less. Not zero. Just less.

So the question is never "is this true". It is:
Who is paying — and what do they get if I believe this?
It works on an advert. It works on a review site. It works on a friend who has just joined something and wants you to join too. And it goes on working when you are forty.
