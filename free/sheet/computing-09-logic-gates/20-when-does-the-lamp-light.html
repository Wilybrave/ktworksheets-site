Which gate, and what goes into it?

  1. A microwave that will only run when the door is shut      you have pressed start.
  2. A car alarm that goes off if the door is opened      a window is broken.
  3. A fridge light that comes on when the door is      shut.
  4. The light on your stairs, with a switch at the bottom and a switch at the top.
  5. A garden light that comes on when it is dark and something is moving — but not in the daytime, however much is moving.
  6. A door that unlocks if you have the card or you know the code, but not if the building is on fire.
