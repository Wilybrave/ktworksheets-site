The Same Water

DIRECTIONS: So why "save water"? Three things run short, and none is the amount.

Clean

Water with nothing harmful in it.

The water that went down your drain is all still there. It is just carrying things now, and taking them back out costs energy and machinery.

Near

Water that is where the people are.

There is an enormous amount of it in the wrong places. Moving it is the expensive part — a pipe, a pump, and a bill.

Now

Water that is here at the moment you need it.

A drought is not a shortage of water on earth. It is rain that arrived somewhere else, or last month, or all at once as a flood.

Clean, near, now.

That is why a country can have a flood and a drought in one year, and why a running tap matters even where it rains: someone spends energy making that water clean and near again.
