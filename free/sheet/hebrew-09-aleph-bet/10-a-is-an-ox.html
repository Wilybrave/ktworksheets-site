Cousins, Not Parent and Child

Hebrew · Page 2 of 2

DIRECTIONS: Read, then answer.
Your alphabet did not come from Hebrew. Both came from Phoenician. Hebrew went one way; Greek went another and became Latin, which you are reading now. They are cousins.
Hebrew kept the old names. Alef still means ox. Greek kept the sound, alpha, which means nothing in Greek. Latin kept only A.
Alpha means nothing in Greek. What does that tell you about where the Greeks got it?
