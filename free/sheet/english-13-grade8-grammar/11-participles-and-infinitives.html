Three verbals, three jobs.
Gerund
a verb naming a thing
swimming is hard
Participle
a verb describing a thing
the running water
Infinitive
to + verb, and it can do either
to run is enough
The ending tells you nothing: broken is a participle too.
  1. The running water was freezing.
  2. Running is cheaper than a gym.
  3. He wants to run the whole loop before dark.
  4. Exhausted, she sat down on the step.
  5. She has a letter to mail.
  6. The broken window let in the cold all week.
