DIRECTIONS: In 1869 Mendeleev's table only worked if he left holes. Read, then answer.
He said the gaps were undiscovered elements and predicted them from their neighbors. For one: density about 5.5, low melting point. Six years later gallium was found: density 5.9, and it melts in your hand.
  1. Closing the gaps would have made a neater table. Say why leaving them was the better science.
  2. A tidy arrangement of things already known is not strong evidence on its own. Say what the gallium prediction adds that tidiness cannot.
