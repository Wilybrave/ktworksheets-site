The Kite — Sharp Folds

Making · Origami · Page 3 of 3

Name Date
DIRECTIONS: Neat origami is all in the folds.
Four rules for a sharp fold.
1. Line the edges up first and hold them.
2. Press the middle first, then out to each end.
3. Go over it again with a thumbnail.
4. Work on a hard, flat table.
  1. Fold three kites. Which was the neatest — the first or the third? Why do you think that is?
  2. Try one deliberately badly: line the edges up carelessly. What happens to the finished kite?
  3. The crease from step 1 is used as a guide for the next two folds. What would happen if that first crease was not in the middle?
  4. Fold a kite with your eyes shut. Which of the four rules matters most?
  5. Draw your kite here, and label the middle crease and the two edges you folded to it.
