The Mouth Is a Hinge

Making · Projects · Page 2 of 6

DIRECTIONS: Cut the oval out, fold it on the dashed line, and glue it inside the mouth.
It is printed the right size. No measuring.
A hinge needs a stop. Folded card gives the mouth a definite open and shut, so it can bite a word.
How it goes in. Fold on the dashed line and press hard. Put the sock on, find the fold your fingers and thumb make, and take it off without losing it. Glue the card along that fold: top half to the fingers side, bottom to the thumb side. Dry flat.
A tongue, if you want one. The tab hides under the fold.
Why bother with a tongue? Because it is the only thing that makes the inside of the mouth worth looking at. A puppet with a tongue has a reason to open wide. A puppet without one is a hole.

Glue the tab to the bottom half of the plate, at the back, so the tongue flops forward as the jaw drops.

Making It Talk

Making · Projects · Page 5 of 6

DIRECTIONS: Three moves, in this order. Practice each one slowly on its own first.
The moveHowWhy it works
It looks.Turn the whole puppet — the head, not just the eyes — toward whatever it is thinking about.This is the big one. A rough puppet that looks at you is alive. A beautiful puppet that stares into space is a sock.
It breathes.Before it speaks, let the mouth open a little and shut again, once, with no sound.Real people take a breath before they talk. Your puppet doing it is what stops it feeling like a hand.
It speaks.Open the mouth on the LOUD part of a word, and only there. Say “po-TA-to” and open once, on TA.Flapping on every letter looks like chewing. One good open per stressed syllable looks like speech.
The test. Say nothing and have the puppet turn and look at somebody. If they look back, it works.
Practice line. Look at the floor, look up, breathe, say: “I have been in that drawer for four years.” Mouth opens twice: DRAW-er and FOUR.
  1. Who is your puppet? Name, and one thing about them nobody would guess.
  2. What is your puppet frightened of? Now have it look at that thing across the room, and write down what its body did.
