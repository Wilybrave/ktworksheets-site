What it does not do
  • No account. Not yours, not your child's. A first name and an age if you want suggestions, and it never leaves the phone.
  • It never goes online. Not "works offline" — a shipped build makes no network requests at all. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no server.
  • Nothing counts you. No streak, no badge, no calendar of the days you missed.
