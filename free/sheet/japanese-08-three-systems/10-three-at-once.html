What You Just Sorted

Japanese · Page 3 of 3

DIRECTIONS: Japanese uses three writing systems in every sentence, each with a job. Read, then answer.
Kanji — the dense blocky ones. Borrowed from Chinese a very long time ago, and they carry the meaty words: drink, watch, person, mountain.
Hiragana — the loopy ones. They carry the grammar: the endings, and the little words that hold the meaty ones together.
Katakana — the sharp angular ones. They spell anything imported from somewhere else. This one says coffee.
