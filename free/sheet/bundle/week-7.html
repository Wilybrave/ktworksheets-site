Solid, Liquid, Gas

The test is not how it feels. It is what happens when you put it in a jar.

Name
Do not go by touch (sand is hard, a towel is wet). Ask: poured into a new container, does it keep its shape or take the container's?
Solid
Shape: keeps its own shape
Space: takes up the same space wherever you put it
for example: a brick, a spoon, an ice cube, you
Liquid
Shape: takes the shape of whatever you pour it into
Space: always takes up the same amount of space
for example: water, milk, honey, petrol
Gas
Shape: spreads out and fills the whole container
Space: takes up as much space as it is given
for example: air, steam, the bubbles in a fizzy drink
Tick one for each. Honey is the one that argues back. It pours so slowly that it looks like it is keeping its shape — but leave it long enough and it will spread out flat and take the shape of the plate. Slow is not the same as solid.

Who Actually Does It?

Tick local or national for each job.

Name
Local — your town or city
A small group of people, usually voted for by the people who live there. They look after the things you can see from your own street.
National — the whole country
A much bigger group, in the capital city. They make the laws that everybody in the country has to follow, wherever they live.
