Asked often

Do I need a printer?

Effectively yes, and that is the point rather than a limitation. Any printer will do — nothing on any sheet needs colour. Every sheet also saves as a PDF you can send to a print shop, a library or a work printer.

Does it work offline?

Completely. All 1,740 worksheets and 396 coloring pages are inside the app, and a shipped build makes no network requests at all.

Is there an answer key?

On most worksheets, printed as its own page after the child's — so a sheet can be handed over without handing over the answers. Sheets whose questions have no settled answer get a different second page instead: what a good answer contains, and the one thing to ask when you get a thin one.

Can I use these in a classroom?

Yes — print what you like, as many times as you like, for your own children or your own class. The terms say it properly.
