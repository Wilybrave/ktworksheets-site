Which Dog Is That?

Field Guide

DIRECTIONS: Twelve breeds, all side on. Not to scale.
Tricky pairs: the first two (long and low), the next two (tall and lean), the next two (pushed-in face).
Dachshund
Longer than it is tall, on legs that barely clear the ground.
Corgi
Low and long too — but a fox’s face, and ears twice the size.
Greyhound
Deep chest, waist tucked right up, built as one long curve.
Great Dane
Lean like a greyhound, but head and shoulders taller than anything here.
Bulldog
Wide and low, front legs bowed out, face pushed flat.
Pug
The same flat face on a small round body, with the tail curled tight.
Chihuahua
The smallest of the lot, and the ears are too big for the head.
Poodle
Clipped into pompoms, with bare legs between the balls of curly coat.
Dalmatian
Spots. Nothing else on this page has them.
Husky
Thick coat, upright ears, and a bushy tail curled over its back.
German Shepherd
The back slopes down towards the tail, and the ears stand straight up.
Labrador
A thick tapering otter tail, carried straight out behind.
