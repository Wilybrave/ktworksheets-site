A Fact About the Eater

What Are You Actually Eating?

Can it make its own vitamin C?
PeopleNoBroken gene. Scurvy is the result.
Guinea pigsNoBroken separately, by a different accident.
Fruit batsNoBroken separately again. All three ate a lot of fruit.
Dogs and catsYesMake their own from sugar. Never need an orange.
GoatsYesMake it in the liver, in large amounts, on demand.
It is a fact about the eater, not the food.

Vitamin C is essential for you, but not for a dog: dogs make their own.

We broke our own recipe.

The gene for making vitamin C is still in you, broken. Our fruit-eating ancestors never missed it. That is why sailors got scurvy and the ship's dog did not.

Nutrients are essential. Foods are not.

Every essential nutrient comes in many foods. Sold a food you must have? Ask: which essential nutrient does it have that nothing else does? Nearly always none.

The nine and the eleven

Protein is built from 20 amino acids. Your body makes 11; the other 9 are essential.

9 you must eat

histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, tryptophan, valine

11 you build yourself

alanine, arginine, asparagine, aspartic acid, cysteine, glutamic acid, glutamine, glycine, proline, serine, tyrosine

You eat protein (meat, beans, bread, milk); your body takes it apart into these pieces and rebuilds its own. A bean can become part of your thumb.
