Three quarter notes and a half note, all at one height, like a drum part.

The same four notes, moved up and down

Same four lengths, same order. Now it is a tune.
A note's position tells you two things.
How far across it is — that is when it happens.
How far up it is — that is how high it sounds.
So written music is a graph: time along the bottom, pitch up the side.
