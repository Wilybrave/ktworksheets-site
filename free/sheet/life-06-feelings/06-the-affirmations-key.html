When something is hard

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 2 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
These are for the middle of a thing, not the end of it.
  • I can do hard things slowly.
  • I am allowed to be bad at something new.
  • I can ask for help and still be doing it myself.
  • Not yet is different from never.
  • I can stop, and come back to it.
  • I can do the first bit and decide about the rest afterwards.
  • Hard is not the same as impossible.
  • I have done things before that I could not do at first.
  • I can do it badly on purpose to get started.
Or write your own.

When something goes wrong

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 3 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
For after the mistake, when the useful question is what happens next.
  • One bad go is one bad go.
  • I can be wrong without being stupid.
  • I can say sorry and still like myself.
  • Getting it wrong is how I find out what I do not know.
  • This will be smaller tomorrow.
  • I can fix some of it, and that counts.
  • I am allowed to be disappointed and still be alright.
  • I can tell somebody what happened.
  • Nobody is keeping the list I think they are keeping.
Or write your own.

When you are scared

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 4 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
None of these says the fear is silly. It is not, and saying so does not help.
  • I can be scared and do it anyway.
  • Scared means it matters, not that I should stop.
  • I can go first for ten seconds and see.
  • I have been this frightened before and it passed.
  • I can take somebody with me.
  • I can say out loud that I am frightened.
  • The waiting is usually worse than the thing.
  • I am allowed to be frightened of things other people are not.
  • My body does this. It does not decide for me.
Or write your own.

When you are cross

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 5 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
All of these assume the feeling is allowed. Sheet 30 is the one about hands.
  • I am allowed to be angry.
  • I can be furious and still choose what I do.
  • I can say I am cross instead of showing it.
  • I can come back to this in ten minutes.
  • Being right does not mean I have to win now.
  • I can be cross with somebody and still like them.
  • I do not have to answer straight away.
  • I can leave the room and that is not running away.
  • The loudest thing I could do is usually not the best one.
Or write your own.

When you are sad

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 6 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
Not one of these tells anybody to cheer up. That sentence has never worked.
  • I am allowed to be sad for as long as it takes.
  • Sad is not the same as broken.
  • I do not have to explain it to be allowed it.
  • I can be sad and still do the next small thing.
  • This feeling has an end even when I cannot see it.
  • I can tell one person.
  • Crying is a thing bodies do, not a thing that goes wrong.
  • I can want to be on my own for a bit.
  • I can ask somebody to sit with me and say nothing.
Or write your own.

When it is too much

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 7 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
Check this page first when everything feels worse than usual.
  • I can do one thing, and then look again.
  • Tired makes everything else louder.
  • I am allowed to stop before I have finished.
  • I can put some of it down.
  • I do not have to decide anything important tonight.
  • Rest is not a reward for finishing.
  • I can ask what actually has to happen today.
  • Slower still counts.
  • I can say I have had enough.

With other people

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 8 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
The ones about the space between you and somebody else.
  • I do not have to be liked by everybody.
  • I can say what I want and still be kind.
  • I can say no.
  • What somebody else thinks of me is mostly about them.
  • I can be the one who says hello first.
  • I can ask instead of guessing what they meant.
  • I am allowed to change who I spend time with.
  • Being left out is a thing that happened, not a thing I am.
  • I can disagree out loud and still be somebody they like.
Or write your own.

About being me

Feelings · The Affirmations Key · Page 9 of 9

DIRECTIONS: Tick two or three you would really say.
These are the slowest ones. Nobody says them once and believes them.
  • I am worth the same whatever I am good at.
  • I can change my mind.
  • I do not have to be the best at it to enjoy it.
  • I am allowed to take up room.
  • I can like something nobody else likes.
  • I am allowed to be proud of myself out loud.
  • I can be a work in progress and still be alright now.
  • I do not have to earn being here.
  • I can be different on different days.
Or write your own.
