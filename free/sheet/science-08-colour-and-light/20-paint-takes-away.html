They add up to white. A screen is only red, green and blue dots.

Together they take away everything. A printer uses cyan, magenta and yellow ink.

The yellow on a screen

There is no yellow on a screen.

Red and green stripes sit side by side, too small to tell apart. Your eye has only three kinds of color detector (red, green, blue), so red plus green fools it into seeing yellow.

Catch it: put a drop of water on a phone screen showing yellow. Look close through the drop. What do you see?
