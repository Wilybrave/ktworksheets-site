It is a letter. Its name is pi, the sixteenth letter of the Greek alphabet, and it says p. Here is the capital:
capital pi
Your P is this letter, turned round with the loop closed. In 1706 a mathematician named the circle number with the first letter of the Greek word for “the way round”, like p for perimeter. It was somebody’s initial.
