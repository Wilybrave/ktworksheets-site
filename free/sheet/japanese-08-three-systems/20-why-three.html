About 1,500 years ago Japan borrowed China’s characters. They didn’t fit: Japanese words change their endings (like walk, walked, walking, only far more), and a character cannot write an ending. So two more systems were built out of the characters themselves.

Hiragana: written fast for centuries until the corners rounded off. That is why it is loopy.
Katakana: one piece snapped off and the rest thrown away. That is why it is sharp.
