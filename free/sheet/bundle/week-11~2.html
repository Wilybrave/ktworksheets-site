Nearly all of it is you.

A cold virus is not big enough or busy enough to make a whole person ache. What it does is get in and copy itself. What makes you FEEL ill is your own body noticing and responding.

The heat is your body deliberately turning itself up, because most germs copy themselves best at your normal temperature and worse when it is warmer. The runny nose is fluid arriving with the things that fight infection in it. The cough is getting the mess out. The aching and the tiredness are chemical signals that make you lie still, which is exactly what a body fighting something needs you to do.

The Ship That Sank in Sight of Land

On 10 August 1628 the warship Vasa left Stockholm harbor on her first voyage. She carried sixty-four bronze cannons on two gun decks, more than any ship then sailing in the Baltic. A light wind came off the shore. Vasa leaned, water poured in through her open gun ports, and she sank after sailing about 1,300 meters. Around thirty people drowned.

An inquiry was held within weeks and nobody was punished: the ship had been built to a design the king had altered himself, and the shipwright who began her had died before she was finished. The wreck lay in cold water too low in salt for shipworms to live in, which is why the timber did not rot. She was raised in 1961, after 333 years on the bottom, and is now the most complete seventeenth-century ship in the world.

It Never Comes Back — What You Just Found

Why there are twelve notes · Page 2 of 2

Twelve steps of 3:2 come to 129.746. Seven octaves come to 128.

They should be the same note. Twelve fifths land 1.36% above seven octaves: about 23 hundredths of a semitone, enough to hear.

It cannot be fixed. Powers of 3/2 are made of threes; powers of 2 are not. They never meet.
  1. Say why this is not a mistake in your arithmetic, a fault in one piano, or something better measuring would fix.
  2. A tuner tunes twelve perfect fifths, one after another, all the way round, and arrives back at the note they started from. What will be wrong with it?
  3. You cannot have both. Describe the two things somebody could choose to do about it, before sheet 20 tells you what was chosen.
