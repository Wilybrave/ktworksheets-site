What a sheet looks like

Written like a good teacher would.

  • 1
    Directions a child can read.One short box at the top says what to do, in plain words.
  • 2
    A worked example first.They see one done before they are asked to do one.
  • 3
    Room to actually write.Big answer boxes and ruled lines sized for a child's hand.

    Easy on your printer.Light lines and no solid fills, so a page uses very little ink.

    The answers are there for you.Every sheet's answer key is in the app.
