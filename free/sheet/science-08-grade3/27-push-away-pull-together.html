Making Magnets Do a Job

Grade 3 · Science · Page 2 of 2

  1. Try it: lay a paper clip on the table and slide a magnet slowly toward it along a ruler. At how many centimeters does the clip jump? Do it three times.
  2. A toy train has a magnet at each end of every car. Two cars push apart instead of joining. What is facing what, and how do you fix it without any tools?
  3. Design a magnet latch that keeps a cupboard door shut but still lets it open with a gentle pull. Draw it, label where each magnet goes, and say which poles face each other.
