Younger Than America — The Date Argues Back

The map is younger than you think · Page 2 of 2

Ask how old Germany is and you get at least three answers.

1871 — the states joined into one country.
1949 — after the Second World War it was divided, and two separate German countries were founded.
1990 — those two joined again, which is when the Germany on today's map began.

All three are defensible. Which one you pick depends on what you think a country is — a people, a government, or a shape on a map — and there is no way to settle that by looking anything up.
  1. Which of the three dates would you use, and why? There is no right answer and there is a right kind of reason.
  2. Say why "how old is this country" is a harder question than "how old is this mountain range".
  3. Somebody says a country is ancient because people have lived there for thousands of years. Say what is right about that and what is wrong with it.
