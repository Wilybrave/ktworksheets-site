Why, and the Rule It Gives You

Making · Projects · Page 4 of 6

DIRECTIONS: Read this after you have raced them.
Speed comes from how far you have dropped. The curve drops early, so its marble is fast almost all the way. This curve (traced by a point on a rolling wheel) is the fastest path there is.
In 1696 Johann Bernoulli set this as a challenge. Isaac Newton solved it in one night and sent it back unsigned. Bernoulli: "I recognize the lion by his claw."
A marble never climbs back to where it started: rubbing takes some of the drop. So every hill must be lower than the one before.
Rubbing means a hill just below the line fails too. Build by the stricter rule: every hill lower than the one before.

A Real One, Down a Door

Making · Projects · Page 5 of 6

DIRECTIONS: Build it all, starting with a steep drop. Then test and fix one piece at a time.
Do thisHowWhy
Cut the tubes in half the long waySquash the tube flat, cut along one fold, open it out, cut along the other. Two half-pipes from one tube.A whole tube hides the marble. Half a tube shows you exactly where it went wrong, which is the only way to fix anything.
Tape it to a door, top to bottomMasking tape, two strips per piece, and press hard. Start at the top and work down.A door is flat, tall, free, and you can take the whole thing off afterwards. Masking tape comes off paint; other tape does not.
Overlap each piece INSIDE the one belowThe top of the lower piece goes underneath the bottom of the upper one, by about an inch.Butt them end to end and the marble hits the lip and stops. Overlap the wrong way and it launches into the room.
Every drop lower than the lastWork down the door and never go back up.Page 4. It cannot climb higher than it started, and it is lower than that already.
Corners cost youSweep them wide. A tight corner throws the marble up the outside wall.Going up the wall is going uphill, and everything it spends climbing it does not get back.
Put a box at the bottomA shoebox on its side, or a bowl.Otherwise the marble goes under the sofa and this is the last time anybody plays with it.
Start steep, so the marble is fast for the rest of the run.
