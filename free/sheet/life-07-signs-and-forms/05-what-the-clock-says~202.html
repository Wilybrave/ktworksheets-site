a.m. or p.m.?

Life Skills · Time · Page 3 of 3

DIRECTIONS: Write a.m. or p.m. for each one.
a.m. is from midnight to noon: the night and the morning. p.m. is from noon to midnight: the afternoon and the evening. A clock with hands looks the same at 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m., so you have to know which part of the day it is.
  1. Everyone is asleep, the middle of the night, at 2:40
  2. Lunch at school, at 12:05
  3. The sun comes up, at 6:25
  4. Bedtime, at 8:15
  5. Breakfast before school, at 7:10
  6. Soccer practice after school, at 4:30
  1. The hour hand is between the 3 and the 4. The minute hand points at the 9. What time is it?
  2. The minute hand points at the 6. How many minutes is that?
  3. It is 3:55. What time will it be in 5 minutes?
  4. The bus schedule says the bus comes at 4:35. On a clock with hands, which number will the minute hand point at?
