What a Timetable Does Not Say

Life Skills · Everyday · Page 5 of 5

DIRECTIONS: The last two have no single right answer.
  1. You get to Elm Street at 8:13 am and the bus has just gone. When is the next one, and how long is the wait?
  2. Somebody tells you "there is a bus every half hour". Look down the Elm Street row and say whether that is true.
  3. Which gap between buses is the longest one on this timetable? Write the two times and the length of the gap.
    Show your work
  4. A timetable tells you when the bus is meant to be there. Write one sentence about what you should do differently because of the word "meant".
  5. You need to be at Hill Top, and you can walk there from The Library in 20 minutes. Does that give you any bus you did not have before? Which one, and why might you still not want it?
