Same sun on land and sea: the land keeps its heat in the top few inches, the sea spreads it deep. The land is hot by lunchtime; the sea has barely noticed. After dark the land cools faster too.

A Prediction You Can Go and Check

Why the Sky Is Doing That

Wind is air evening out a temperature difference.

  1. One patch gets hotter. Because of the angle, or because it is land and next to it is water.
  2. The air over it rises. Warm air is thinner for its size, so it floats up through the cooler air around it.
  3. Less air is left pressing down. The weight of air above that patch drops. That is what LOW PRESSURE means.
  4. Air slides in from beside it. From wherever there is more, towards wherever there is less. That sideways flow IS the wind.

If the chain is right, a coast breeze blows in off the water in the afternoon and the other way before dawn.

Test it.

On a still, sunny day at a coast, check a flag or a wet finger in the afternoon, then late evening. No swing? The chain is wrong, or bigger weather got in the way.
