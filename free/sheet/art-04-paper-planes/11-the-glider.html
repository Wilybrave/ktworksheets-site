The Glider — The Folds

Making · Paper planes · Page 2 of 3

DIRECTIONS: Any sheet of paper works. Each picture shows the paper before the fold.
1.
Fold it in half the long way, then open it out again.
2.
Fold the top left corner in to the middle crease.
3.
Do the same with the top right corner.
4.
Fold the sharp point straight down, so the tip meets the bottom of the two triangles.
5.
Fold the whole thing in half, along the crease from step 1.
6.
Fold one wing down, wide. Turn it over and match the other.
✓
Finished, from above. Wings fold down on the dashed lines. Hold it underneath and throw it level.
Set the wings in a shallow V, not flat. Dives? Bend the back edges up a little. Stalls? Bend them down. Turns? Check the folds match.
