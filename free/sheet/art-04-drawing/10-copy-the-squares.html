A circle, square by square. Nobody draws a good circle freehand — but a circle crosses each grid line at a spot you can find, and the piece inside each square is a short curve you can copy. Mark the crossings first. Join them afterwards.

The head is one big circle. Find where it crosses each grid line first, then join those points.
