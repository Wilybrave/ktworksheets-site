DIRECTIONS: 24 things worth saving, and what each one is actually good for.
One box, with a lid. If it does not fit in the box, it does not get saved.
Out of the recycling
WhatWhat it is good at
a paper towel tubealready the strongest shape there is. Legs, columns, anything that gets stood on
a toilet paper tubethe same, shorter. Wheels, hinges, rollers
a cardboard boxthe flat sides are the best building material in the house — stiff, free, and cuts with scissors
a cereal boxthin card. Bends without snapping, which cardboard does not
a plastic bottleholds water, and the neck screws — the only threaded thing you get for nothing
bottle topswheels, feet, counters. Almost the only round flat things in a house
a milk cartonwaterproof, and the folded top is a hinge already
a shoeboxthe box for all of this, and a stage or a room when you need one
Worth keeping a handful of
WhatWhat it is good at
strawsthe cheapest way to make a triangle, and triangles are what stop things wobbling
popsicle sticksstiff, straight, and they take glue. The nearest thing to timber
clothespinsthe only clamp you have. Hold glued things while they dry
elastic bandsstore energy. Anything that flings, snaps back or drives itself
stringpulls but never pushes — same as a muscle
split pinsthe only joint that turns. Everything that moves needs these
marblesweight in a small space, and they roll, which is two different jobs
buttonssmall weights, small wheels, and they have holes already

From the drawer
WhatWhat it is good at
tapefast, weak, and undoes. Use it while you are still deciding
masking tapetakes pen, peels off, and is what to label things with
white glueslow, strong, and wants clamping. Use it once you are sure
a glue stickpaper to paper only. It will let you down on anything thicker
scissorsthe sharpest thing this pack ever needs
a hole punchmakes a round hole in the right place, which is hard any other way
a rulerfor straight lines and for scoring folds — press hard with a closed pen
modeling clayweight you can move around. The whole of the balancing sheet
Do not bother saving
WhatWhy not
yoghurt potsthey squash the moment anything rests on them, and everybody saves forty
egg boxestwo is plenty. They are only ever bumps, and you already have bumps
anything that smelled of foodit still does, in a box, in a cupboard, in July
foil trayssharp edges when cut, and they crumple instead of folding
sweet wrappersthey look like treasure and are structurally nothing at all
more than three of anythingif you have four, you are collecting rather than saving
Scissors only, with a grown-up in the room. If a step seems to want a knife, snip or fold instead.
