At the Table

For the grown-up · Page 1 of 2

DIRECTIONS: Mealtime signs. Use them at every meal.
MILK Hold a fist up and squeeze it open and shut, the way you would milk a cow.
When: the obvious one, and often the very first sign a child gives back.
WATER A W hand — three fingers up — taps the chin twice.
When: the cup that is not milk. Worth having both so they can choose.

And the Polite Two

For the grown-up · Page 2 of 2

DIRECTIONS: Two polite ones.
PLEASE Flat hand circling on the chest.
When: attached to anything they are asking for. Goes well after more.
THANK YOU Flat hand, fingertips at the chin, moved forward and down.
When: when they get it. You will make this one far more often than they do at first.
Please is a flat hand circling on the chest. Make a fist instead and it means sorry.
