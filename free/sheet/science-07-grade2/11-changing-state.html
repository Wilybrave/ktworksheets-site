Prove the water is still there
  1. Put a little water in a cup and mark the level with a pen.
  2. Put a clear plastic bag over the top and hold it on with a rubber band.
  3. Leave it somewhere warm and sunny for a few hours.
  4. Look at the INSIDE of the bag.
