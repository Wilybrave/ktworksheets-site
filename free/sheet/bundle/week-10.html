The Land Does the Explaining

Grade 5 · First peoples · Page 2 of 3

DIRECTIONS: Give the reason for each answer on page 1. It is nearly always the land.
People build with what is there: wood where forest was thick, earth or hide where there were no trees. Where corn grew, people stayed and built towns; where the food moved, so did they.
  1. Why did people on the Plains live in homes that could be taken down and moved?
  2. Why did people of the northwest coast build large permanent wooden houses?
  3. Corn, beans and squash were grown together across much of the continent. What does growing your own food let a people do that hunting alone does not?
  4. Nations traded shells, copper, obsidian and food over thousands of miles. What does that tell you about how connected they were?

Grade 5 · First peoples · Page 3 of 3

DIRECTIONS: Given the land, work out the life.
A place you have never heard of. Wide grassland, no trees for a hundred miles, hot summers, hard winters, and enormous herds of grazing animals passing through twice a year.
