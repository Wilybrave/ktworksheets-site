A finger switch has two ends, and someone has to press it.

Give it a third wire, so current in that wire closes it. The oldest kind is a relay: the third wire is a coil that turns into a magnet and pulls an iron arm across. The first computers used thousands.

What just happened

The thing that flips the switch is now the same kind of thing that comes out of one: electricity. So one switch’s out can work the next one’s flip, in as long a chain as you like, with no finger anywhere. The rest of this pack is this one idea, stacked.

A transistor is the same thing with nothing moving inside: in, out, and a third wire that decides. With nothing to move, it can flip billions of times a second.
